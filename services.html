MICROWAVE REPAIR

Microwave Repair Service

Inspection and repair support for solo, grill and convection microwave ovens, subject to model and spare-part availability.

  • Microwave running but not heating
  • Sparking, smoke or unusual sound
  • Turntable plate not rotating
  • Keypad, display or control-panel fault
  • Door lock, fuse and internal component issue

  • Burner not lighting or low flame

  • Gas leakage inspection and servicing
  • Built-in hob installation and cleaning
